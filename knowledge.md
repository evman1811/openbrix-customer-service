## What tlyfe is
- tlyfe is a tenant app made by OpenBrix (Bristol, UK), built with the deposit scheme TDS. **VERIFY**
- It covers the renting journey: finding a home, referencing, moving in, living there, moving out. **VERIFY**
- tlyfePRO is the version for letting agents: managing tenant relationships, communications and onboarding. **VERIFY**

## Tenant features
- Digital ID verification and Right to Rent checks. **VERIFY**
- Affordability calculator. **VERIFY**
- Interactive move-in process and automatic storage of tenancy documents. **VERIFY**
- Record of rent payments that tenants can add to their credit history (credit builder). **VERIFY**
- Deposit protection handled through the app, with TDS. **VERIFY**
- Marketplace deals / concierge services and a low-cost tenant loan option. **VERIFY**
- Free for tenants of TDS member agents. **VERIFY**
